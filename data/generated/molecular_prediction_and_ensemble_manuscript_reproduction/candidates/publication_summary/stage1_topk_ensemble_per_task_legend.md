# Figure legend

**Stage-1 chemical ensemble selection across ensemble sizes.**
Held-out test area under the precision-recall curve (AUPR) is shown for Top-K
equal-weight chemical ensembles for each Tox21 endpoint. Candidate models were
ranked independently within each endpoint, and predictions from the
highest-ranked K models were averaged. The filled marker and vertical dotted
line identify the selected ensemble size, K=4. Y-axis limits are independently
scaled by endpoint because AUPR depends on class prevalence.
