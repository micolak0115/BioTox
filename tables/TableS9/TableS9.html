Table S9

Context-specific predictive performance of chemical and transcriptome-complemented models across the twelve Tox21 endpoints. AUPRC is reported to three decimal places for Chem. and Chem. + Bio. on matching held-out compounds within each endpoint, cell line, and exposure time. The 6-h rows represent primary full cohorts; the 24-h rows represent paired exposure-time cohorts. The frozen chemical logit is calibrated on each training partition; this intercept is held fixed while transcriptomic β coefficients are fitted using unweighted BCE plus a ridge penalty selected by inner cross-validation. Unadjusted perturbation expression is standardized using training-fold means and standard deviations. Bold Chem. + Bio. values and asterisks denote nominally significant positive improvements over the corresponding Chem. value (*P < 0.05, two-sided Nadeau–Bengio corrected resampled test, without multiple-testing adjustment). Endpoint labels are bold when at least one displayed context meets this criterion. † denotes fewer than 20 active compounds. Paired-cohort 6-h results are retained in the full numeric source but are not displayed.
